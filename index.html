Privacy Policy – Auriontik

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Auriontik ("we", "our", or "the app") is a focus timer and productivity app for Android. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's features:

  • Email address – collected when you create an account via Firebase Authentication. This is required only to sync your premium subscription status across devices. Account creation is optional; you can use the app without signing in.
  • Premium subscription status – whether your account has an active premium subscription, stored in our Firebase database.
  • Session and usage data – your focus timer history, streak count, daily study log, and settings are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data does not leave your device unless you are signed in, in which case only your subscription status is synced to our servers.
  • Purchase token – when you subscribe, Google Play Billing provides a purchase token to RevenueCat so we can verify and manage your subscription. We do not store or process payment card details.

We do not collect your name, phone number, precise location, contacts, or browsing history.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and authenticate your account and keep you signed in across sessions.
  • To verify and restore your premium subscription across devices.
  • To process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing via RevenueCat.
  • To store your focus timer preferences and session history locally on your device.
  • To generate personalized AI motivation tips (premium feature) — only your session subject, duration, and streak count are sent to OpenAI's API; no personally identifiable information is included. OpenAI does not use this data to identify you personally.
  • To improve app stability and diagnose crashes (aggregated, non-identifiable data only).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Notifications

If you enable study reminders, we schedule local push notifications on your device. These notifications are generated entirely on-device — no notification content is transmitted to our servers or any third party. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's system settings or within the app's notification preferences.

4. Firebase Services

Auriontik uses Firebase, a platform provided by Google LLC, for the following services:

  • Firebase Authentication – manages account creation, sign-in, and session tokens using your email address.
  • Cloud Firestore – stores your premium subscription status so it can be restored when you sign in on a new device.

Firebase processes data in accordance with Google's privacy policies. For details, see: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Data in transit between the app and our Firebase services is encrypted using TLS. Data at rest in Cloud Firestore is protected by Firebase Security Rules that allow each user to access only their own records.

Your focus session history, streaks, settings, and timer state are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers. You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app or using the in-app account deletion option described below.

Account deletion: You can permanently delete your account directly within the app at any time:

  • Go to the Profile tab.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm the two-step deletion dialog.

This immediately and permanently removes your Firebase Authentication credentials, your Firestore database record, all locally stored data on the device, your RevenueCat customer identity, and all scheduled notifications. This action is irreversible.

If you are unable to access the app, you may request deletion by contacting us at the address in Section 7. We will process your request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Auriontik is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it promptly.
